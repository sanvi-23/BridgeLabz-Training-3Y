A practical look at fonts, spacing, weight, and emphasis.

Why Typography Matters

Typography influences how quickly and comfortably readers understand content. A carefully selected font family, size, and weight creates a clear visual hierarchy.

Good typography also depends on contrast, consistent spacing, and a comfortable reading rhythm. These choices make information easier to scan.
